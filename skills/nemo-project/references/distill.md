# Preserve findings and process inputs

Apply the knowledge rules loaded through the entry skill. Work only on the relevant, understood set of inputs and findings; leave unrelated arrivals alone.

1. Read existing subject owners and necessary source material. Record unreadable or partially processed material and its impact; it remains pending. A request only to keep or file an artifact preserves it without forcing extraction.
2. Merge information that improves future work into the owning subjects, preserving evidence, meaningful failures, decision rationale and unresolved ideas with their status. A deliverable alone does not preserve reusable knowledge. Apply the shared navigation and consistency rules to the affected records.
3. Read back retained claims against their sources before cleanup. Verify useful information survived, uncertainty remains visible, affected deliverables agree and the knowledge is retrievable. Repeating with no new information leaves state unchanged.

## Retention and cleanup

Explicit retention and authorization instructions take precedence. Move processed retained originals out of inbox/ into sources/ or its mapped equivalent, preserve bytes and update references. “Keep unchanged” preserves content, not the intake location; an explicit instruction to keep the original at its path overrides moving. Compare colliding names before filing; preserve distinct material without overwriting it.

Disposable inputs and generated intermediates become eligible for deletion only after complete processing and preservation checks. Delete when the current authorization permits cleanup; if an applicable rule requires approval, finish preservation and report exactly what remains pending. This procedure does not grant destructive permissions.

Keep uncertain or unprocessed material, durable deliverables and maintained components. Cleanup never extends to external originals, repositories or unrelated user work. Remove superseded task detail only after preserving unique useful content. Do not create archives or discard useful evidence to meet a size target.

Done means relevant knowledge is supported and retrievable, not that the inbox is empty. Report unresolved processing, conflicts or cleanup briefly.
