
# PKM work
Read the selected library's relevant index, existing notes and local rules before changing
content. Let those rules determine note scope, location, links, evidence and review state.
Keep raw sources distinct from maintained notes where the library does; preserve uncertainty and
the user's decisions. For a read-only question, answer from the relevant notes without creating
files. Follow provenance to verify living-source facts when the answer needs current state; notes
and cached excerpts cannot establish it. If the source is inaccessible, disclose the limit and use
retained content only as historical or unverified evidence. Immutable evidence and user decisions
need no age-based refresh. For requested note work, save the result in the library's authorized draft
location and check its local format and links. Promotion or publication requires its own authorization.

Default to stable references and retrieval cues for living sources. Keep useful local analysis and
decisions with provenance; copying values needs a purpose and snapshot status in the library's
existing format. Preserve originals and protected sections. Compare identities and relevant content
before adding or updating notes: equivalent state requires no rewrite, renamed duplicate or prompt.

For maintained subject notes, write the insight, procedure or decision directly with brief
provenance; keep processing stories and test-run history in their existing evidence role. This does
not change the library's treatment of journals, biographies, original sources or personal sections.
